One Chapter · Slow Reading

One short, dense chapter a day. Tomorrow is locked until tomorrow.

Some books are not meant to be finished in a weekend. Vladimir Tarasov’s Технология жизни is a sequence of parables; Machiavelli’s Prince is twenty-six compact arguments. Reading three in a row is easy; living with one overnight is the point.

  1. Split by essay, not by volume. Bring the EPUB, MOBI, or FB2 you own, or start with a public-domain classic in the shared library.
  2. A calendar, not a queue. Receive one chapter each morning on Telegram and in the quiet web reader. Miss a morning and it waits in your archive without piling up two chapters the next day.
  3. A line to keep. Record a brief reflection on today’s reading before closing the page.
